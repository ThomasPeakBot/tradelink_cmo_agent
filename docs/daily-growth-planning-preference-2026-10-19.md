# Standing daily planning preference — October 19, 2026

The owner requests at least 1–2 tasks every day focused on growth activities and experiments. Target two concrete growth tasks within the existing 4–5-hour daily budget; always include at least one, explaining any constraints that prevent two.

Prioritize acquisition, activation, conversion, retention or referral work using stored company knowledge, metrics and experiments. Routine reporting, administration and data cleanup do not count toward this growth focus. Each task should include a clear deliverable, measurable outcome, step-by-step instructions and copy-ready AI prompts. Experiments should specify a hypothesis, audience/channel, success criterion and review point. Fit task selection to actual blockers, evidence and available time.

This is an ongoing planning preference, not approval to publish, send outreach, spend money or execute external actions. Existing Today tasks and recorded outcomes are preserved; apply the preference to future generated plans.
